Who we are

A community built by engineers, for engineers.

.NET Hub Kathmandu is a vibrant tech community dedicated to Microsoft's .NET technology stack. We exist to bring developers together — to share knowledge, collaborate on real projects, and stay current as the .NET ecosystem evolves.

Our mission is simple: foster collaboration, inspire innovation, and contribute to the growth of Nepal's technology industry. Every session is run by senior software engineers and Microsoft MVPs who volunteer their time to mentor the local developer community.

  • Hands‑on workshops — from ASP.NET Core APIs to Umbraco CMS
  • Local Microsoft Build & GitHub Copilot Dev Days watch‑parties
  • Official .NET Foundation community group
  • Free, open, and welcoming to developers of every level
